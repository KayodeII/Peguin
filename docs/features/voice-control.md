# Voice control ("Hey Peguin")

A virtual mouse on the owner's Mac. Default is Clicky-style hold-to-talk: hold Control+Option, speak, release to run. Wake-word mode ("Hey Peguin …") is optional. Peguin's cursor glides to the target and it clicks, types, scrolls, opens things or draws on top of the screen. Only the owner's own mic, heard on the Mac by whisper. No AI and no screenshots for clicking: buttons are found by name through macOS Accessibility. Off by default (Settings > Voice control).

- **Say:** "click sign in", "show numbers" then "click 7", "click this" (where the mouse is), double-click, right-click; "open my browser", "go to github.com", "open Slack", "close Music" / "quit Spotify", "search for ..."; "scroll down a bit", "go back", "new tab", "close tab", "reload", "copy", "paste", "undo"; "type hello there", "press enter", "find pricing"; "draw a circle / an arrow here", "box sign in", "highlight this", "draw" (freehand, "done" or Esc), "clear"; "stop".
- **Trigger** (`pointer.trigger`, default `hold`): **hold** — Control+Option down listens, release runs the command (`ptt-on` / `ptt-off` in the Swift helper; PCM is gated until the keys are down; `createListener.flush` ends the utterance on release). **wake** — "Hey/OK Peguin, ..." or the press shortcut (default Control+Alt+Space). **both** — either. Wake words, shortcut and search address are settings when wake is in play.
- **Where the owner finds it:** the **#cursor** channel in the sidebar (marked ON or OFF) has the switch, the permissions, how the cursor looks, things to say and Try it. Settings > Voice control keeps how to talk to it, Smart or Quick, the search address and asking the AI, with a link back.
- **Permissions:** Microphone, and Accessibility (#cursor shows both, with Allow). Accessibility is required for clicks and for the Control+Option watch. Without it, opening sites, apps and searches still work in wake mode.
- **Smart drive** (`drive: "smart"`, the default; `desktop/src/main/pointer/drive.ts`, pure): opening, finding, clicking and searching are planned by the owner's AI one step at a time. Each step: `targets {dock: true}` (Dock, the menu bar, then the front window; an open menu's items are read and listed first, so a step can pick Quit or any other menu item), `observe` numbers them, `drivePrompt` asks for one JSON step, `readStep` accepts only ids it was shown, and Peguin does it with its own cursor. Guards: 12 steps at most, Esc or "stop" ends it, `canType` refuses typing unless a text field was just clicked or something new appeared (keys would land in whatever app is in front; Spotlight can't be seen), `stuck` stops a step that changed nothing, and the prompt forbids sending, deleting, buying or posting unless asked. "Open Music" skips the AI: `openApp` clicks it in the Dock (or heads there and opens it when a full-screen app hides the Dock). "Close Music" does too: `quitApp` goes to its Dock icon (`dockLabel`: the Dock's name can differ, "iTerm2" for iTerm), right-clicks it and clicks Quit in the Dock menu (or asks the app to quit when there's no Dock icon), so it can still ask about unsaved work; every helper click first moves the mouse onto the spot, since menus ignore a click on an item the mouse hasn't moved onto; the AI has the same `quit_app` step. Everything except the instant kinds (`INSTANT`: scroll, keys, typing, drawing, clear, numbers, stop) and apps named as installed goes to the AI. Each step it also gets the running apps (helper `apps`), the open browser tabs (`tabs.ts`: each browser's AppleScript, titles and sites only, so "close my Gmail" is `close_tab`, not quitting Chrome) and the owner's last five requests from the past ten minutes (for "close it"). It can `ask` a question; the owner's next words, without "Hey Peguin", go back into the same task. End of speech is 900 ms so a pause mid-sentence doesn't cut a command off. A new request stops a drive still in progress. Silero VAD runs inside whisper-server (`--vad`, `desktop/resources/ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin`, shipped with the app): measured 2026-10-21, synthetic hiss, hum, music and typing clips came back as "Okay." and "." without it and empty with it, spoken commands word for word either way, and 2.5 minutes of the owner's room went from a filler line every few seconds to none. Whisper's own confidence (`no_speech_prob`, `avg_logprob`) rated invented filler as sure as speech, so it isn't used. Wake word (`pointer.wake`): "name" (default) or "hey", where "Hey" on its own wakes Peguin (`wake(text, names, hey)` marks it `bare`), never while the copilot is in a meeting. Attention (`listenS`, default 15 s, stretched while the owner keeps talking; `followS`, default 30 s): unsure lines (a bare "Hey …", the line after a bare "Hey", a follow-up) that aren't a known command go to the AI as a quiet drive with a note (`UNSURE.greeting` or `UNSURE.followUp` with the last request); nothing shows until it picks a real step, and "not a request" goes back to idle silently. Measured 2026-10-21: "Hey" … 12 s … "open calculator" opened it, "now close it" quit it, "Hey John, how was your weekend?" was dropped. A scrap ("Hey Peguin, I'm...", `fragment`) arms and keeps listening instead of going to the AI; an answer to Peguin's question goes to the AI marked as possibly someone else (a TV), to be dropped if it doesn't fit. The first caption of a drive is what Peguin heard, so a mishearing shows at once. Whisper's loops on noise ("oh, oh, oh, oh") are dropped, and "Hey Peguin. Hey Peguin" counts as the name once. Development: `PENGUIN_OVERLAY_SNAP=1` saves the top of the overlay as `overlay-<mode>.png` in the profile on every mode change (the overlay's own window, so no Screen Recording needed). The helper reads the front app through the system-wide accessibility element; NSWorkspace's goes stale without a run loop. Measured: "open chrome and look up emperor penguins" took 5 steps, about 9 s each with Claude.
- **Your AI for misheard commands** (`askAi`, on by default): a line addressed to Peguin that `parse` can't place goes, as text only, to the owner's AI (`interpretCommand` in `brain.ts`), which answers with one command from the grammar or NONE; a Claude CLI is kept warm while voice control is on. Polite padding (`unpad`) and several clauses in one breath are handled before that, and app names are matched to installed apps (`matchApp`). Smart drive and interpret share `brain.ts` fallback: a session/rate limit on the chosen AI tries Codex then Grok if they're set up on this Mac; if none can answer, a short "out of uses" line appears in a box under Peguin's cursor.
- **Peguin's cursor**: a black penguin head (beak = hotspot, an eye that looks where it's going and then follows the owner's mouse); it wanders around the middle of the screen away from the owner's mouse (`roam.ts`) and goes to look at the window the work log just noted. Fin and Arrow are options; the colour is the glow.
- **Learn my voice** (Settings > Voice, off by default): lines that aren't commands go to `learnFrom`, which keeps clean ones (4 to 15 s, 8+ words, mostly speech, not distorted, the owner's daily limit) for review. Never while the copilot or a meeting is live.
- **The work log** (Sources > On this Mac) uses the same helper (`front`) and the same Accessibility permission for window titles.
- **While the copilot is in a meeting**, a command waits 1.8 s and is ignored if the meeting audio said the same words (it came through the speakers).

## Try it from source

```bash
cd desktop && npm run setup:pointer && npm run build
PENGUIN_POINTER_TRY="show numbers;draw an arrow here" npx electron .
```

Accessibility from source: macOS asks about whichever app is responsible for the process. Started with `npx electron .` from a terminal, that's the terminal (or the tool running it), not Electron. To test clicks, start it as an app and allow "Electron":

```bash
open -n -a desktop/node_modules/electron/dist/Electron.app --args "$PWD/desktop"
```

Electron from npm is ad-hoc signed, so its permission is tied to that exact copy; after reinstalling `node_modules`, run `tccutil reset Accessibility com.github.Electron` and allow it again.

`PENGUIN_POINTER_TRY` (development only) runs those commands 2.5 s apart once voice control is on. Settings > Voice control also has a "Try it" box.

## Files

| Path | Role |
|---|---|
| `desktop/src/main/pointer/commands.ts` | Pure: `wake`, `parse` (the commands), `matchTarget`/`labelScore` (a spoken name to a button), `commandPrompt`, `isFiller`, `Echo`, `describe`, `searchUrl` |
| `desktop/src/main/pointer/tabs.ts` | Pure: the AppleScript that lists, closes and switches browser tabs (Chrome's family and Safari), and reading its output |
| `desktop/src/main/pointer/index.ts` | `VoiceControl`: the mic listener, hold-to-talk, the Swift helper, overlay windows, running each action, numbers, freehand, the echo wait |
| `desktop/native/pointer.swift`, `desktop/scripts/build-pointer-helper.sh` | `pointer-helper`: glide, click, scroll, type, keys, Control+Option PTT watch (`ptt-on`/`ptt-off`), the front app's clickable elements, the default browser, the camera notch's size (so the island wraps around it); JSON lines over stdin/stdout |
| `desktop/src/renderer/pointer.tsx`, `desktop/src/preload/pointer.ts` | The see-through overlay (#pointer): Peguin's cursor, captions, listening orb, badges, drawings, the Dynamic Island (wrapped around the camera notch on Macs that have one); mic capture on the primary display |
| `desktop/src/renderer/cursor.tsx` | The #cursor channel |
| `desktop/src/renderer/voicecontrol.tsx` | The switch and permissions, the cursor settings, things to try (used by #cursor), and Settings > Voice control |
| `desktop/src/main/speech/whisper.ts` | `createListener` takes a `prompt` (voice control's own) and a `rate` (the overlay records at 24 kHz); utterances carry their audio |
| `desktop/src/main/copilot/session.ts` | Tells `echo` what the meeting said |

## Tests

`test/pointer.test.ts`: the wake phrase (including talk about penguins and the owner's own wake words), every command family, numbers and URLs, button matching (exact beats partial, ties become choices, nested duplicates count once, no guesses), fillers and the prompt's own words. Measured: spoken clips through the real listener and whisper-server (24 of 24 commands, no actions from 8 ordinary sentences or noise, ~0.4 s transcription), and on screen in the app (numbers, captions, a drawn arrow, the "which one?" choice). Not yet: the owner's own voice on a real mic, clicks in Chrome and Safari pages, a second display, freehand drawing, the echo wait in a real copilot call.
